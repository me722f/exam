You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a good credit score (typically 620+), a down payment saved (usually 10-20% of the home price), and you plan to stay in the area for at least 3-5 years. Our team can help assess your financial readiness and connect you with mortgage pre-approval services.

Spring and early summer (March through June) are traditionally the best times to sell. Buyers are more active, and homes tend to sell faster and for higher prices. However, market conditions, location, and your personal situation all play a role. Our agents can help you determine the optimal time to list your property.
